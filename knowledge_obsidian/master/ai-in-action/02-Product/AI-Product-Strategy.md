# AI Product Strategy
**Day 16 · Track 1** · Market positioning & JTBD

## Blue Ocean vs Red Ocean
| Blue Ocean | Red Ocean |
|------------|-----------|
| Create new demand | Compete in existing market |
| Low competition | High competition |
| Make competitors irrelevant | Beat competitors |
| *Example: AI search disrupting web search* | *Example: another chatbot* |

## JTBD (Job-to-be-Done) Framework
Core question: *What job does the user hire your product for?*

**Job Story format**: When [situation], I want to [motivation] so I can [expected outcome].

## Switching Costs
Users only switch when new solution delivers **10× value**.
- AI disruption changes what users expect — incumbents that don't adapt lose.
- *Chegg*: paid homework help → free AI answers destroyed business.

## AI Leverage Points
1. Where is user slow/wrong in current workflow?
2. Can AI augment (keep human in loop) or automate?
3. Is the cost-of-error low enough for full automation?
